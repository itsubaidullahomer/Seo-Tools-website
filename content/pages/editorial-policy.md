This page explains how the tools and written content on {{siteName}} are produced, so you can judge how far to trust them.

## Who is responsible

{{siteName}} is built and maintained by {{authorName}}. {{authorName}} decides what is published and is responsible for its accuracy. Questions and corrections go through the [contact page](/contact).

## How tools are built and tested

- Every tool is software that runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is uploaded to our servers.
- Before a tool is published, its results are compared with independently calculated values, including edge cases such as empty input, very large input, negative numbers and unusual characters.
- Tools are checked on desktop and mobile screen sizes in a real browser.
- When we describe how a result is calculated, the explanation on the page matches what the tool actually does.

## How written content is made, and how AI is used

The explanations, worked examples and guides on this site are drafted with the help of AI writing tools. We say so openly. AI drafts are then checked before publication:

- **Numbers and formulas** are verified by calculating them independently, not taken on trust.
- **Standards, platform limits and policies** (for example character limits or file-format rules) are checked against the official source where we can reach it, and pages show a "last updated" date so you can see how fresh the information is. Where something could not be confirmed, the page says so instead of stating it as fact.
- **Filler and repetition** are removed. Each page should teach something specific to that tool.

AI can still make mistakes, so please treat figures that affect money, health, legal or safety decisions as a starting point and confirm them with a qualified professional. See the [disclaimer](/disclaimer).

## Independence

We do not accept payment to change how a tool works or what a guide says. Advertising appears on the site and is clearly labelled; advertisers have no influence over content. If affiliate links are ever added, they will be disclosed on the page where they appear.

## Corrections

If you find a mistake, tell us through the [contact page](/contact) with the page address and what is wrong. Confirmed errors are fixed promptly and the page's "last updated" date changes. Significant corrections are noted on the page.

## Updates

Platform rules, limits and tax or legal conventions change. We review pages when we learn something has changed or when a visitor reports it, and we update the date shown on the page when we do.
