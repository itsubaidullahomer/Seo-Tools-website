We love hearing from the people who use {{siteName}}. Here is how to reach us and what to include so we can help quickly.

## Report a problem with a tool

Please tell us:

- the **name of the tool** or the page address,
- **what you did** (the input you used, if it is not confidential),
- **what happened** and what you expected instead,
- your **browser and device** (for example "Chrome on Android" or "Safari on Mac").

Because the tools run in your browser, this information helps us reproduce the problem on the same setup.

## Suggest a new tool or feature

Tell us what you were trying to do and where existing tools let you down. Suggestions that come with a real use case go to the top of the list.

## Content corrections

If a guide contains an error or something that is out of date, send us the page address and the sentence in question. We correct confirmed mistakes promptly.

## Advertising, partnerships and press

For business enquiries, please include your company name and website. We do not accept paid links, sponsored "reviews", or requests to place hidden content.

## Privacy requests

To ask about personal data, see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) first – in most cases we hold no personal data about you at all, because the tools do not send your input to us.
