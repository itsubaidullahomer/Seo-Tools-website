### 2026 context that changes the picture

1. **Zero-click and Google widgets.** Zero-click searches are about 68% (first four months of 2026). Google shows built-in widgets for: basic calculator, unit conversion ("cm to inches", "kg to lbs"), currency ("15 USD to GBP"), **tip calculator** (confirmed, with bill split), random number generator, roll a die, flip a coin, colour picker (a hex code shows a colour card), metronome, and time zone answers ("time in London"). Head terms for these lose many clicks. Tool pages still earn clicks for multi-step or bulk use (unitconverters.net still has about 2.4M organic).
2. **AI Overviews.** One 2026 dataset puts the traffic loss for tool/calculator queries at only about 3%, much lower than for informational content. Interactive tools are one of the most AIO-resistant page types, which supports the whole strategy.
3. **Scaled content abuse (March 2026 core update).** Google named scaled content abuse explicitly. Sites with thousands of template pages lost 60-90% of traffic. This matters for "90 days from today", "EST to PST" and "20% off 50" style programmatic pages. **Recommendation:** build ONE strong tool per intent, and add at most a small number (5-20) of hand-written preset pages only where the SERP shows dedicated pages ranking (for example "90 days from today", "est to pst"). Each preset page needs genuinely different content. Never publish thousands of variable-swap pages.
4. **YMYL.** Health (BMI, TDEE, pregnancy, ovulation, body fat) and finance (mortgage, loan, paycheck) are Your-Money-Your-Life topics. AI Overviews appear on about 88% of healthcare informational queries (BrightEdge Feb 2026). SERPs are held by NHS/CDC/Mayo/BabyCenter/NerdWallet/Bankrate. A new domain without medical or financial authors will struggle. The CPC is high, but the ranking odds are low.

### AdSense / policy notes for this category

- Calculators and converters are low-risk AdSense content in general.
- **Pregnancy / due date / ovulation.** AdSense sensitive categories cover "sexual function, fertility, birth control... STDs and abortion", but explicitly **"not normal pregnancy resources"**. A due-date calculator is therefore fine. An ovulation/fertility calculator sits next to the fertility category: advertisers in that category may be filtered by default, and personalised ads are restricted under the Google Ads "Health in personalized advertising" policy (fewer ads that pay less). Content must avoid any medical claims. Flag: **low-medium**.
- **Love calculator.** Allowed if framed as entertainment only (lovecalculator.site runs AdSense). Do not collect or store names; add an "entertainment only" disclaimer. Flag: **low**.
- **Finance calculators.** Allowed. Add a "not financial advice" disclaimer. Do not imply lending or brokerage.
- **Currency converter.** No policy risk, but not feasible without a rates API. Free ECB-based APIs exist (for example frankfurter.app), which would mean a live third-party fetch. Mark as roadmap / server-light.
- **Random number generator / dice.** Fine, but **do not frame as lottery-number picking or gambling** (for example "lucky lottery numbers generator"). That would touch the gambling exclusion.
- **Blood alcohol (BAC) calculator.** It appears in the easycalculation top-50, but it is **excluded**: it can be read as help to decide whether one can drive after drinking. That is a liability and alcohol-sensitive, so it is not worth the risk.
- **GPA / grade calculators.** Fine. These are not "homework cheating".

### Percentage calculator (hub) + change + off
