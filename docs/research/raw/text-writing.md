## 1. Headline findings (read this first)

1. **The category is enormous and proven monetizable.** convertcase.net (a one-person case converter site, 50+ small text tools) does ~5M visits/month (Semrush, May 2026) and publicly reports ~$20,000/month revenue from display ads on ~2.3M pageviews with ~$20/month costs (indiehustle.co, Hacker News thread 39119230). wordcounter.net does 3.2M–4.6M visits/month. textfixer.com (tiny, old-school text-tools site) does ~870K visits/month. wordstotime.com (single words-to-minutes tool) does ~180K visits/month, 64% from Google organic.
2. **Head terms are high-competition but not brand-dominated the way image/PDF tools are.** Page 1 for "word counter" / "character counter" / "case converter" is mostly mid-size tool sites (wordcounter.net, charactercounter.com, convertcase.net, capitalizemytitle.com DR 71) plus Grammarly/QuillBot free tools. A new domain will not take #1 in 6 months, but these pages are needed as topical hubs and they collect long-tail traffic.
3. **Best opportunities for a new domain = "utility fixes" and "question-intent calculators":** remove line breaks, remove duplicate lines, alphabetize/sort lines, comma separator (column → comma list), whitespace remover, slug generator, characters↔words converter, words↔pages calculator, reading time / words-to-minutes. Page 1 for these is thin pages on small sites (pinetools, codebeautify, textfixer, delim.co, alphabetizer.flap.tv, sortmylist.com 38K visits/mo). 
4. **Unicode "text style" generators are a large, low-authority cluster** (small text, bold text, strikethrough, upside down, cursive, zalgo/glitch, invisible character). lingojam.com gets 144K visits/month from "font generator" alone at position #2; fsymbols.com has 2.4M US visits/month; upsidedowntext.com 181K. Competitors are thin, ad-heavy pages. Risk: pages that are only a converter with no explanation are exactly what AdSense calls "Low Value Content" — each page needs real explanatory content (how Unicode math alphanumerics work, where they render, accessibility caveats).
5. **US/UK/CA/AU share:** capitalizemytitle.com is 66% US; lingojam 31% US + 3.7% UK + 3.6% CA; wordcounter.net 40% US. Case converter / text compare / textfixer audiences skew India/Bangladesh (lower CPC). Writer-oriented tools (word counter, reading time, words to pages, title case, syllable counter) skew Western.
6. **Infeasible for launch (AI/API needed):** grammar checker, paraphrasing tool, text summarizer, plagiarism checker, AI detector, spell checker. paraphraser.io does 726K visits/month, editpad/toolsaday/smallseotools all lead with these — we cannot compete there without an LLM backend. Documented but not recommended.
7. **AdSense/policy context (2025–2026):** the #1 AdSense rejection reason is "Low Value Content"; tool pages that show only a widget are explicitly cited. Guides recommend 1,500–2,000+ words of genuinely explanatory content per tool (how it works, who it is for, examples, FAQs). Google's March 2026 core update explicitly targeted "scaled content abuse" (sites with hundreds of templated AI pages lost 50–80% traffic). Implication: do NOT mass-generate hundreds of near-duplicate "how many words is N characters" pages; build a handful of strong calculator pages with tables instead.

## 3. Competitor tool inventories (text category only)

**textfixer.com** (867K visits/mo): remove line breaks, add line breaks, alphabetical order, word frequency counter, sentence counter, uppercase/lowercase converter, replace text online (find & replace), random choice generator, random sentence generator, random word generator, reverse text generator, remove white spaces, remove duplicate lines, HTML tools (strip HTML tags, Word to HTML), image to PDF. All JavaScript, in-browser.

**convertcase.net** (5M visits/mo, 100+ tools): case converter (upper/lower/sentence/title/alternating/inverse), title case converter, sentence case, find and replace text, Unicode text converter (bold/italic/script/monospace), duplicate line remover, strikethrough text generator, reverse text generator, mirror text generator, ASCII art generator, plus code formatters, encoders, image converters. Has browser extension + mobile app (offline).

**wordcounter.net** (3–4.6M visits/mo): word counter, character counter, website page word counter, alphabetize tool (alphabetize/randomize/format list), edit counter, random word generator, auto capitalize sentences, words per page calculator, citation generator, grammar/spell check.

**capitalizemytitle.com** (1.3M visits/mo, DR 71, 66% US): title capitalization (AP/APA/Chicago/MLA), character counter, word counter, alphabetizer, remove line breaks, column to comma-separated list, small/tiny text generator, bold text generator, strikethrough generator, cursive text generator, fancy text generator, twitter character counter, morse code translator, online tally counter, random word/verb/pictionary/name generators, plus programmatic "How many words is 500 characters?" (/character-count/500-characters/) and "How long does it take to read 1000 words?" (/reading-time/1000-words/) pages.

**lingojam.com** (~8K global rank): fancy text generator, font generator, bold text generator, bold font generator, small text generator, zalgo text, mirror your text, hundreds of user-made translators.

**10015.io**: case converter, lorem ipsum generator, letter counter, text to handwriting converter, whitespace remover, bionic reading converter, URL slug generator, font pair finder (+ dev tools).

**editpad.org / toolsaday / smallseotools / prepostseo / duplichecker**: lead with AI paraphraser, summarizer, grammar checker, plagiarism checker, AI detector, essay writer — all infeasible client-side. prepostseo also has invisible character, reverse text, text to handwriting.

**Other single-tool winners worth copying:** wordstotime.com (words → minutes, 180K/mo), alphabetizer.flap.tv (alphabetize), sortmylist.com (38K/mo), delim.co (comma separator), linebreakremover.com, removelinebreaks.net, upsidedowntext.com (181K US), fsymbols.com (2.4M US), textreverse.com (reverse text + text repeater), wordhtml.com (Word → HTML, 630–820K/mo), syllablecounter.net, howmanysyllables.com, morsecode.world (288K/mo from one keyword).

## 6. AdSense / Google policy notes (2025–2026)

- **"Low Value Content" is the dominant AdSense rejection** (adsenseaudit.net, adstimate.com, Medium "AdSense Rejection Fixes 2026"). Explicit call-out: "A calculator or converter tool that simply shows the tool interface without explaining how it works or when to use it is considered low value; Google expects how the calculation works, who should use the tool, examples, and guidance." Recommendation seen repeatedly: 1,500–2,000+ words of genuinely explanatory documentation per tool, plus About/Contact/Privacy pages and clear authorship.
- **Google Search spam policies:** "Scaled content abuse", "site reputation abuse", "expired domain abuse" (March 2024) with enforcement waves Jan 2025, Aug 2025 spam update, and the **March 2026 core update** that explicitly targeted scaled content abuse (sites with hundreds/thousands of templated AI pages lost 50–80% traffic; one site with 1,800 auto-posts lost most traffic in 9 days). Method-agnostic: intent and value matter, not whether AI was used. **Implication:** avoid mass-generating hundreds of near-identical calculator sub-pages (e.g., 500 "how many words is N characters" pages). A single strong page with a lookup table + a handful of hand-written high-volume variants (500, 1000, 1500, 2000 characters; 500, 1000, 2000 words) is the safe pattern.
- **Unicode "fancy font" pages are AdSense-monetized in practice** (igfonts.us and ultratextgen.com state they serve Google AdSense). Risk is not the topic but thinness: ensure explanatory content (Mathematical Alphanumeric Symbols block, rendering/compatibility table, accessibility warning that screen readers spell out or skip these glyphs).
- **Category-specific cautions**
  - *Text to handwriting:* competitors market it as "create assignments online" — that is academic-dishonesty framing. Frame as notes, cards, design mockups, fonts demo. Medium risk if mis-framed.
  - *Text repeater:* commonly used for message spam. Keep copy neutral, no "spam your friends" language. Low risk.
  - *Email extractor:* only "from pasted text"; never "scrape emails from websites" (spam/harvesting framing). Low risk with correct framing.
  - *Invisible character:* used for blank game names; platforms tolerate it (Fortnite since 2022). Low risk; avoid "bypass filters" language.
  - *Essay word counter:* fine as a counter; do not market as essay writing help.
  - *Everything else in this category:* no policy risk.
- **Hard exclusions from the brief are not touched** (no downloaders, proxies, DRM, adult, gambling, fake documents, click fraud). Nothing in this list scrapes copyrighted content; all tools operate on text the user pastes.
